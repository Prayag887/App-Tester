# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want App Tester to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or skill level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent and give actionable feedback.
- Protect privacy: never post credentials, certificate keys, device identifiers,
  or unredacted captured traffic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, personal
attacks, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository's security contact or to a
maintainer. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
